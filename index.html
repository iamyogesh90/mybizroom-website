ABOUT MYBIZROOM

One workspace for your everyday business operations.

Small businesses often manage customer conversations, orders, payments and follow-ups across different tools. MyBizRoom brings these everyday activities together in a structured business workspace.

MyBizRoom is designed for businesses that use channels such as WhatsApp and Instagram to communicate with customers, while keeping their business operations organized in one place.
